OPTION 1: MASTER CRON (HIGHLY RECOMMENDED - EASIEST & ALL-IN-ONE)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
In your cPanel -> Cron Jobs, add just ONE cron job running every minute (* * * * *):

Command:
* * * * * php /home/USERNAME/public_html/cron_master.php >/dev/null 2>&1

(Replace /home/USERNAME/public_html with your actual server document root path)

What this master cron handles automatically:
- WinGo 1 Min (every minute)
- 5D Lottery 1 Min (every minute)
- K3 Lottery 1 Min (every minute)
- TRX WinGo 1 Min (every minute)
- WinGo 30 Sec (runs every 30 seconds)
- WinGo 3 Min, 5D 3 Min, K3 3 Min, TRX 3 Min (every 3 minutes)
- WinGo 5 Min, 5D 5 Min, K3 5 Min, TRX 5 Min (every 5 minutes)
- WinGo 10 Min, 5D 10 Min, K3 10 Min, TRX 10 Min (every 10 minutes)
- Agent Daily Salary & Spin Reset (daily at midnight 00:00)

OR via Web Cron (e.g., cron-job.org or curl):
curl -s "https://bharatgames.online/cron_master.php?key=bharatgames_cron_2026" >/dev/null 2>&1
